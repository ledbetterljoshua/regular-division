# Regular Division

**Live:** https://ledbetterljoshua.github.io/regular-division/

What if M.C. Escher had a collaborator who could compute? Six interactive rooms, each one a thing he reached for by hand and couldn't finish — made live, interactive and infinite.

| Room | What it does |
|---|---|
| I · Seventeen | Draw a stroke; it's replicated by any of the 17 wallpaper groups. |
| II · Three Geometries | One shader for (p,q,2) triangle groups on the sphere, the plane and the Poincaré disk. Paint the cell. |
| III · Endless Metamorphosis | A tessellation strip that morphs forever; every edge is shared, so it can never gap. |
| IV · The Hole in the Middle | An original scene through the conformal Droste map; twist the slider to see the tear. Upload your own image. |
| V · Einstein | A verified spectre (aperiodic monotile) substitution tiling with an editable edge. |
| VI · Impossible Objects | A Penrose stair, tribar and crate in 3D that close from exactly one viewpoint. |

All motifs and scenes are original. Escher's prints are under copyright; nothing here recreates one — only his techniques and the mathematics underneath them.

Plain static HTML, no build step. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder.
