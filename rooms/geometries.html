Three Geometries

Escher tiled the flat plane, carved creatures onto wooden spheres, and late in life built his Circle Limit prints by compass, ring by ring toward an edge he could never reach. One rule, the triangle group (p, q, 2), makes all three. Paint one cell and the geometry does the rest.
