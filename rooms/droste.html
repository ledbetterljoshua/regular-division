The Hole in the Middle

In Print Gallery (1956) a print swells around until it contains the gallery it hangs in, but Escher could not resolve the centre and left a blank patch with his monogram. In 2003 Hendrik Lenstra and Bart de Smit showed the picture follows a conformal Droste map, a twisted complex logarithm, and computed how the centre should be filled. Here it is filled, all the way down.
