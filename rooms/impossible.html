Impossible Objects

In 1958 Lionel and Roger Penrose published an impossible staircase and an impossible tribar in the British Journal of Psychology; Escher built prints on both around 1960–61. On paper an impossible object only has to be drawn from one viewpoint. Built in 3D it is a real object that closes from exactly one — here it is built, and your eye is free to move.
